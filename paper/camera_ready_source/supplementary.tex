\noindent
The ``UNSTABLE'' verdict in the first table refers to the \emph{full three-way}
ordering, which does not reproduce in any of the three settings. That is not in
tension with the main paper's cross-domain claim, which is about a
\emph{partial} order: MobileNetV3-Small ranks below both larger backbones on
every seed in both field settings. The full ordering is unstable everywhere
because it inherits the instability of the ResNet-50 against EfficientNet-B0
comparison, which the main paper declines to resolve.

across seeds.

\section{Training Curves}

Every seed is drawn rather than averaged. Early stopping fires at different
epochs, so a mean curve over epoch index would silently truncate to the earliest
stop and misrepresent the runs that trained longer.

## What Core Dataset V1 is

Immutable image identities, the labels used for classification, the train/test splits, source provenance, duplicate exclusions, conservative uncertainty exclusions, leakage decisions, effective manifests and dataset fingerprints.

Its readiness deliberately does **not** depend on action mappings, the harm matrix, risk weights, treatment recommendations, or risk-aware evaluation approval. Those belong to the Risk Evaluation Layer, and whether a leaf is correctly identified and split is not answered by what treating it would cost.
